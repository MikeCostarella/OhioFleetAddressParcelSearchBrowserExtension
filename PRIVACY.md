# Ohio Parcel Finder: privacy

- **What leaves your browser:** only the address you search for. It is sent to
  the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder (`geocoding.geo.census.gov`) to find its
  county and location. The resulting parcels-map link opens on
  `mikecostarella.github.io`, carrying that address and point in its URL.
- **What is stored:** the last address you searched, in the extension's own
  local storage on your device, so the popup can pre-fill it. Nothing else.
- **What is not collected:** no browsing history, no page contents, no
  analytics, no accounts, no cookies of our own. Text on a page is used only
  when you select it and choose *Find Ohio parcel* from the right-click menu.
